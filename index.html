Jakophil YT Uploader

Jakophil YT Uploader is a private tool used only by the owners of the Jakophil YT YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

The tool connects to the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API in order to:

  • upload videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to our own channel,
  • read performance data (views, watch time, revenue reports) of our own channel,
  • read and, after manual approval, reply to comments on our own videos.

It does not access any other user's YouTube account or Google data.
