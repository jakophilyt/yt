Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy describes how Jakophil YT Uploader ("the app") handles Google user data. The app is a private tool used only by the owners of the Jakophil YT YouTube channel.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's consent, the app uses these Google OAuth scopes for the channel owner's own account:

  • YouTube (manage videos, playlists and comments of our own channel),
  • YouTube upload (upload videos to our own channel),
  • YouTube Analytics and YouTube Analytics monetary reports (read-only reports for our own channel).

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish and manage videos on our own channel and to analyse its performance. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with third parties.

How the data is stored

OAuth credentials are stored as protected environment variables in a private, access-restricted environment. Analytics figures may be kept in private files and spreadsheets of the channel owners. No data of other YouTube users is collected beyond public comment text on our own videos.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Compliance

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube API Services is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
